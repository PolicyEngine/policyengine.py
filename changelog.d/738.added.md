`pe.us.calculate_household` accepts a list of groups for `marital_unit`, `family`, `spm_unit` and `tax_unit`. Each group is a mapping with `members`, a list of indexes into `people`, plus its own inputs, and each person belongs to exactly one group of the entity. An entity passed as a list comes back as a list of results in the same order, with one axis series per group under axes. A mapping still puts every person in one group, so existing calls return the same results. Callers can now give a household with children or other adults its own marital units: policyengine-us reads a two-person marital unit as a married couple in its SSI and Medicaid rules, so one marital unit for everyone gave a single parent with one child the couple's Medicaid income limit and counted a child's earnings as their married parents' SSI income.
