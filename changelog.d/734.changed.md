`create_datasets` and `ensure_datasets` (UK and US) check years requested for the certified default dataset (no dataset, its name, or its certified URI) against the supported years the bundle manifest records, before any download, and `ensure_datasets` does so before reusing year files that already exist, without reading the source or the year files. A US year file that an earlier release created for an unsupported year of `populace_us_2024`, such as 2022, 2023 or 2036, is now refused rather than reused. Other datasets, and certificates without the record, keep the check computed from the source; `allow_unsupported_years=True` still turns both checks off. Adding the record changes the bundle manifest's bytes, so both TRACE sidecars were regenerated, and the US certificate digest (`certificate_sha256` of `data_releases.us`) changes.
