Qualify each new policyengine-uk wheel or uk-data release automatically before promotion. The new `certify-uk-release` workflow and `scripts/check_uk_candidate.py` resolve and deduplicate the candidate pair. They build a candidate commit with the manifest, pins, lock, TRO and test constants regenerated together, and run mandatory gates on it, none of which can be skipped: installed versions, wheel hashes, core compatibility, data access, dataset bytes, supported years, Budget inputs, the reform contract and metric review bounds. The metric review reads the aggregate-impact tool's small-cell format: a figure from fewer than 10 records is never compared, a change that would come from one goes to review, totals rebuilt without different suppressed figures are lined up from published figures where they are sums, a skipped check counts as not run, and only reports in that format are written to the ledger. From the reform contract's population checks, which run on the survey data, the ledger publishes each row's check, route and status and only the true/false findings of its detail. They also send policyengine-scorecard an immutable replay request, record a qualification receipt in the `uk-qualification-ledger` branch, and promote in order. Certification gains an exact `qualified_exact_pair` basis backed by the ledger receipt, together with a core compatibility check. The release dispatch now carries the certified UK bundle identity and goes to the Budget dashboard and the scorecard as well.
