A UK `Simulation.run()` now stamps its output with the stamp of the year file its input data was cut into, if any, and an output format revision. A UK dataset read from a year file, or returned by `create_datasets`, keeps the file's stamp in `metadata`. `Simulation.load()` refuses a saved UK output, and `Simulation.ensure()` runs it again, when the simulation's dataset is read from another year file or another cut of it, or when the year file the output was calculated from was cut with another year-file format revision or other policyengine-core or policyengine-uk versions than are installed. A UK year file cut again because the bundle re-pinned its dataset, its local source changed or a package was upgraded therefore no longer leaves outputs calculated from the old cut in use. The error names the years, sources or versions that differ. UK outputs saved before the stamp existed are calculated again once.
