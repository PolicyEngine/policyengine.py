`compute_uk_geography_disclosure` now keys regions on ONS GSS codes (`E12000001` to `E12000009` for England's regions, `W92000004`, `S92000003` and `N92000002` for Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland), whether the household output carries `region_code_oa` or only the stored `region`, so releases from national and local datasets publish the same codes. Microcosm's pseudo codes for the nations map to the country codes, `UNKNOWN` is unassigned, and any other region value raises. Region codes in `parent_of` and in `prior_public` must be GSS codes; the table is `UK_REGION_GSS_CODES` in `policyengine.outputs.uk_geography_disclosure`.
