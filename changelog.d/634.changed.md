The UK qualification replay gate applies policy d1344-replay-regressions-v1. It fails lost results, lost responses (a measure-year the candidate lists as inert, or a measure-year total of £0 against a base at least the diff's bound away), mapping flags and incomplete grids. It sends every other flagged move to a named reviewer. Each receipt records the policy id and its text's SHA-256. Automatic UK promotion is on (d1344): a qualified candidate still built on current main is moved onto main, by a push leased on its parent, once every check of its own runs on its own tree has passed; runs of other pull requests at the same commit, forks' included, are not counted. Refresh re-runs a candidate's failed, timed-out or cancelled runs up to four attempts within GitHub's 30-day window, and a candidate left waiting on a run it will not re-run fails the promote run with an error naming it.
