A US `Simulation.run()` stamps its output with the register of renamed stored inputs it was calculated under, an output format revision, and the stamp of the year file its input data was cut into, if any. `Simulation.load()` refuses a saved US output, and `Simulation.ensure()` runs it again, unless that stamp matches this release exactly. An output calculated before a rename was registered, or from a year file that is out of date, is therefore not reused. The error says why, naming the renames the output may lack. Outputs saved before the stamp existed are calculated again once.
