Updated the UK Budget reform contract to version 1.1.0. Its rate-blending case, `mid_year_blended_rate`, now holds capital gains realisations fixed (both elasticity conventions zero and no separate Business Asset Disposal Relief elasticity), so its static expected values hold whatever realisation response policyengine-uk applies by default. Added `cgt_retention_response`, which checks the response itself: with a retention-rate elasticity of 1, a 20% basic rate from 6 April 2027 scales realised gains by (1 - 20%) / (1 - 18%) on every route. `capital_gains_response()` builds a case's elasticity changes.
